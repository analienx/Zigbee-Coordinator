## Open problem and V57

**Intermittent cold-start failure.** The host and the SLZB lose power nightly (about 23:30 to 06:05). On 1, 2, 10 and 11 Nov, Zigbee2MQTT got no `SYS_PING` reply on its first three starts and started on the fourth, with the radio up, fault-free and never reset.

**Likely cause (not proven).** TI's MT frame parser (`npi_frame_mt.c`) waits without limit for length + 3 bytes after any `0xFE`, and accepts lengths 251–255. One stray `0xFE` plus a length byte on the RX line around power-on swallows the host's next frames. Replaying Zigbee2MQTT's start-up bytes into TI's unmodified parser, a stray length of 82–86 reproduces the 11 Nov pattern exactly and no other length does. The stray bytes themselves were never captured.

**V57** drops a stale partial frame after an idle gap, refuses impossible lengths and records what the parser discards (B1E8). It also removes all dimmer-specific code. Details, validation and the pre-flash checklist are in the [release notes](RELEASE_NOTES.md). After the flash, read live evidence after each morning start, before any radio restart, to confirm or rule out the cause.
