### What remains unproven

The **separate intermittent cold-start SYS_PING issue** occurred on 4 of the preceding 11 mornings under V56. V57's idle-gap resynchronization and B1E8 parser evidence were designed to diagnose and prevent it. The immediate clean V57 boot proves firmware loading, startup and live service, but **does not prove** the previously intermittent problem is eliminated. Inspect B1E8 on later real cold boots *before any radio restart* and record whether the first-start SYS_PING succeeds.

Long-duration reliability, independent hardware review and rollback qualification remain distinct open gates. Neither an R4 firmware image nor an operator patch makes stock-vs-R4 superiority universal across all loads or devices.
