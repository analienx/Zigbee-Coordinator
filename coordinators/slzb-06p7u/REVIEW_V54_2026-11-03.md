## Health of V54

The firmware is healthy in steady state.
- **Stack:** 0.8–2 % busy per hour. No saturated window, no onset record.
- **Heap:** at most about 10 KB of the 97.4 KB OSAL heap ever used (low water 87.3 KB).
- **Task stacks:** Stack task 1280 / 3064 B, NPI task 408 / 1016 B.
- **NPI, MT and UART:** no fault and no transport HOLD. Every admitted SREQ reached MT.
- **Timers (V52):** no allocation failure. At most 2 deferred, 12 active.
- **NV read cache (V53/V54):** 99.2 % hit rate, about 520,000 reads served from RAM in 7 h.
- **NV:** 100–700 ms of NV time per hour, longest single operation 120 ms, no failure.

## V55 candidate

Release notes: [RELEASE_NOTES.md](RELEASE_NOTES.md).

**Firmware (one function).** `firmware/slzb-06p7u/r4/b1/tools/patch_security.py`, `TCLK_INIT` (`APSME_TCLinkKeyInit`):
- After the ordinary +11 boot reservation, the row whose EUI-64 is `0xa4c13850cfcdb3a4` gets TX = max(TX, `0x10400000`). That is 272,629,760, about 3.1M above the highest baseline counter and far from the wrap guard.
- RX is unchanged. The row goes through B1's checked write, then the RAM copy, so the checkpoint invariant (RAM ≥ NV) holds.
- It only raises and only for that EUI. Every later boot adds the usual +11 and nothing else.
- This follows the vendor-sanctioned pattern of setting the TC link key's outgoing APS counter for device restoration. TI's fresh-join reset itself stays as upstream, which is spec-consistent (new key, new counter space).
- Remove it once the dimmer runs firmware that clears stale key pairs on migration.

**MAC queues (R4).** `tools/r4_t6_full_capacity.py stage-opts` rewrites TI's pinned `MAC_CFG_TX_DATA_MAX=5`, `TX_MAX=8`, `RX_MAX=5` to 50/80/50, exactly once, and refuses anything else. `audit-native` re-checks the effective defines. Buffers are heap-allocated on demand, and the live heap low water is 87 KB free.

**Composed C diff against installed V54:** exactly those 12 lines in `zd_sec_mgr.c`, plus the 4-byte build digest. Every other TI file is byte-identical; the MAC change lives in the compiler options.

**Tests.**
- B1 hosted suite on the real TI 8.33 sources (ASan/UBSan): 309 passes, 37 compiled mutants rejected.
- New scenarios: `tclk_restore_floor`, `tclk_restore_above_floor` (normal +11 only), `tclk_restore_other_peer` (one EUI bit differs). Each also checks the next boot is idempotent.
- New mutants: `tclk_restore_any_peer`, `tclk_restore_lowers`.
- Full local composition of every stage and harness passes.
- Repository suite: 961 passed, 31 skipped.

**Delivery.**
- `FRESH_REJOIN` acknowledgement in `stock20260311_security_preflight.py` and `p7u_paired_binding.py`. It allows exactly TI's reset: same slot, EUI, key type and install-code index; VERIFIED → DEFAULT (0xff); SeedShift+1 mod 16; counters restart.
- `KEY_REROLL` does not cover it, and neither does another device's acknowledgement. Covered by tests.
- The V55 package must carry `{"ieee": "0xa4c13850cfcdb3a4", "change": "FRESH_REJOIN"}`.

**Tooling.** `tools/p7u_live_evidence.py` and its one-shot extension read B1E1–B1E7 and B1S1 through the running owner with no outage:
- RAM-only B1Q1 queries inside herdsman's request mutex.
- No NV, reset, BSL or radio action.
- The extension is removed afterwards.
