# SLZB-06P7U status, 18 November 2026: V58 stable, hangs resolved in the field

**Verdict:** R4 **V58** has run on the production coordinator since **16 Nov 2026 10:37** without any coordinator hang, and V57/V58 together since **13 Nov 21:36**. Both failure classes behind the hang reports are gone from the field data:

- **Cold-start adapter failures** (`SRSP - SYS - ping after 6000ms`, several Zigbee2MQTT attempts per morning): last seen 13 Nov 06:14, before V57. Five nightly cold starts in a row since then (14–18 Nov, two of them on V58) connected on the **first attempt**.

This is field evidence over about 4.7 days (2.2 days on V58), not a guarantee. The [hang runbook](HANG_RECOVERY_RUNBOOK.md) stays the first response if a hang ever recurs.

## Findings from V58's new evidence

1. **Route-discovery refusals: fixed.** With the table at 40, every herdsman recovery discovery succeeded. HallBulb failures that remain are `MAC_NO_ACK` on the last hop (weak link), not refusals.
2. **UART receive loss: confirmed, rare.** B1E9 counted one hardware overrun, followed seconds later by the parser's first resync of the day. The resync recovered without any host-visible failure. Larger NPI RX buffers (32 → 128 B) are the candidate for V59; no rush.
3. **B1E9 occupancy figure is wrong.** It reads 39–40 of 40 "live" entries even immediately before discoveries that succeed, so TI's unused-entry marker is not `expiryTime == 0`. The success/refusal counters come from the radio's own status and are unaffected. Fix in V59; not worth a flash on its own.
