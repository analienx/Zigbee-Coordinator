## How it got there

Three earlier authorized attempts held **before any write**. Each time the runner restored Zigbee2MQTT on the unchanged V53, with about 2 minutes of outage.

| Hold | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `RUNTIME_INVENTORY_DB_PIN` | The DB pin still expected the retired custom host's approval file. | The DB is admitted when it equals the quiesced cold archive taken before the flash. |
| `NATIVE_SECURITY` (`ADDRESS_MANAGER_RETAINED_ROWS_CHANGED_NO_MIGRATION_AUTHORIZED`) | Normal network churn since the V53 baseline: one device joined, one gained a direct association to the coordinator, two re-derived their trust-centre link key. | Owner acknowledgements `NEW_PEER`, `ASSOC_GAIN` and `KEY_REROLL`, pinned per device in the package ([details](OFFICIAL_OWNER_DELIVERY.md#resilience-prediction-automatic-restore-owner-acknowledgements)). `tools/p7u_addrmgr_diff.py` shows the row diff, and preflight now predicts both gates before taking any outage. |
| `SECTOR_SCOPE` | V54's source was not pinned to the 0x34000 APP envelope that V53 uses. | Pinned there, in the native priority-seal check and in the startup evidence sets ([checklist](OFFICIAL_OWNER_DELIVERY.md#pinning-a-new-firmware-source)). |

The `NEW_PEER` device is an IKEA RODRET remote (E2201). It joined during a permit-join on 1 Nov, passed its interview, failed to configure (bind timeout), and was force-removed from Zigbee2MQTT a minute later. Force removal leaves the coordinator's address-manager row and link key in place. That leftover row is harmless (1 of 406 slots). Re-pairing the remote reuses it.

## Operations

- **Auto-recovery:** the HA automation `z2m_p7u_auto_recovery` ([YAML](ha/z2m_p7u_auto_recovery.yaml)) restarts a stopped Zigbee2MQTT, up to 3 attempts, the later ones after an SLZB radio-only restart, and checks the MQTT round trip. The runner turns it and the boot automation off for every flash or hang window.
- **SLZB Ethernet watchdog:** [`slzb/eth_watchdog.be`](slzb/eth_watchdog.be) installed on the board on 1 Nov in **dry run**. It logs `DRY RUN would reboot now` instead of rebooting. Check the script log after the next overnight router outage, then set `DRY_RUN = false` ([install notes](slzb/README.md)).
