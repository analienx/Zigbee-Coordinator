# R4 V57 production field audit — 14 November 2026

**Status: serving, no observed coordinator hard hang during this sampled boot.** Evidence is from **one physical SLZB-06P7U R1.73 / CC2652P7** over USB, with the official Zigbee2MQTT **2.14.2-1**, at **12:17 Prague time (11:17 UTC), 14 Nov 2026**. Firmware identity is the physically installed **R4 V57** source `eb59b6773dd0fe328c1194647c00e8a070d18a22`, established by the live B1 firmware-source digest. This is field evidence, not a controlled stock-versus-R4 benchmark.

> **One-line result:** a dense **106-device mesh (57 routers, 49 end devices)** has remained serviced by this V57 boot for **6 h 12 min** with no stack saturation, NPI/UART write fault, B1 NV failure, timer allocation failure, or MAC *request-admission* refusal. Five **runtime MT resynchronizations**, 9,320 MAC data-confirm failures, and Zigbee/ZCL errors are recorded and remain distinct from a coordinator hard hang.

### V57's resynchronizations: a useful result, not proof of the original cause

- First resynchronization occurred **18,869.9 s after boot** (about **11:19 Prague**), in **DATA** state, declared frame length **75**, with 67 bytes already collected. Last was **20,031.2 s after boot** (about **11:39 Prague**).
- The first valid MT frame after boot occurred at **254.7 s**, **before any recorded resync**. `boot_discarded` was empty.
- That means the firmware did **not** encounter the original hypothesized stray-byte/cold-start framing state on this morning's boot. The five later resyncs demonstrate an active recovery mechanism but **do not prove** they prevented a hard hang. Their cause, and the dropped 363 bytes, merit investigation against OTA / host traffic.
- The `500 ms` idle boundary in V57 deliberately abandons incomplete frames; a resync is therefore not equivalent to a CRC-corrupted frame or a successfully recovered ZCL command.

### Remaining non-coordinator issues

- The broader Zigbee2MQTT log contains `NWK_NO_ROUTE`, `NWK_TABLE_FULL` (two observed lines), end-device response timeouts, and `UNSUPPORTED_ATTRIBUTE` when polling devices after reconnect. Some endpoint devices remain difficult to reach or are not fully compliant.
- **9,320 failed MAC confirms** (11.69% of request confirmations) should be examined as link/routing load, not hidden behind the zero-refusal count. No proof currently ties them to a coordinator-level hang.
- Our one-shot `SYS 0xF1` B1Q1 diagnostic queries cause Zigbee2MQTT/herdsman to log `CommandID '241' from subsystem '1' not found` at 12:17. The direct diagnostic capture did return valid decoded replies. These errors are **associated with the custom diagnostic interface, not an observed ZNP outage**, and the compatibility/noise issue is a follow-up.
- Home Assistant SMLIGHT management HTTP connection failures are separate from a proven USB coordinator transport fault.

## Why this firmware is materially different from stock

The decisive high-load source correction is **V53's guarded NV read cache**. Before it, per-frame NV accesses were repeatedly traversing persistent NV and overwhelming ZNP service under heavy traffic. After V53, live V56/V57 evidence shows approximately **99.9% cache hits**, with the NV scanning hot path removed for cached reads. This is the **highest-confidence explanation for the major responsiveness improvement in this installation**; it should be highlighted ahead of the later queue and diagnostic enhancements.

Supporting hardening: V43–V50 bounded SRSP/serial reply progress, V52 made OSAL timer enqueue O(1) and cleaned expired timers, V54 added cache-HOLD/security exclusions, V55 increased MAC queues from 5/8/5 to 50/80/50, and V57 added the bounded MT idle-gap resynchronizer and B1E8 evidence. The independent B1 guards preserve NV/network/security state. See [stock comparison](../../docs/R4_VS_STOCK.md), [V53 full source review](REVIEW_V53_2026-10-31.md), [V57 release](RELEASE_NOTES.md), and [13 Nov delivery receipt](STATUS_2026-11-13.md).

**Claim boundary:** there has been **no hard hang observed during this one 6 h 12 min monitored V57 session**. The previous V56 morning cold-start failure was intermittent, and a single successful V57 morning cannot prove permanent elimination. There is no controlled stock-versus-R4 lifetime benchmark, and the sampled 57-router topology is not a verified 60+-router count. Network errors and parser resyncs were **not zero**. Document subsequent cold starts before declaring the issue permanently closed.
